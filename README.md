# ⚔️ Daily Survival Quest

**A simple app that turns your day into a dungeon you have to clear.**

---

## Why this exists

Most habit trackers fail for the same reason: they are *lists*. A list asks nothing of you. You open it, you see fourteen grey boxes, you tick three, you close it. Nothing happened. Nothing was at stake.

Daily Survival Quest is built on a different idea — the one that makes leveling-up stories so compelling. **The day itself is the dungeon.** It opens at midnight, it closes at midnight, and it does not wait for you. A notification arrives. You accept it, or you don't. What you do inside those hours is recorded, and what you fail to do is recorded just as honestly.

That framing changes something real. When "read for twenty minutes" is item nine on a list, it is a chore. When it is a quest inside *Training*, and your rank is sitting at C, and clearing it moves you to B — you do it. Not because the app tricked you, but because you finally gave the small, invisible, easily-skipped things the weight they always deserved.

The things that actually shape a life are never urgent. Getting up when you said you would. Half an hour on the work nobody is asking about. Holding your tongue when you had every reason not to. The small kindness no one would notice you skipping. These never announce themselves. They have no deadline. They are exactly the things that quietly disappear from a life that isn't watching.

**This app watches.** Not to shame you — the tone is deliberately encouraging, and a bad day is still just a day. But at the end of a month you will have something almost nobody has: an honest, day-by-day record of who you actually were, instead of who you remember being.

> *"Every day is a gate. Clear it, and you rise. Waste it, and it is never returned."*

---

## What makes it different

**One gate, one day.** You cannot tick tomorrow's boxes today, and by default you cannot go back and quietly fix yesterday. The day is either cleared or it isn't. This is the whole point — a tracker you can edit freely is a diary you can lie to.

**It costs you nothing and asks nothing of you.** No account, no subscription, no server, no internet. One file, opened in your browser. Your record never leaves your machine.

**It is entirely yours.** Nothing is hardcoded — not the name, not the quests, not the wording of the notification, not the colours. You write it. The app just holds you to it.

## Features

### The gate
- A landing screen with your dungeon's name, crest and tagline, over a blurred calendar
- **Today's cell glows** and pulses on the calendar; every other day is locked
- Tapping today raises a **system notification** — accept it, and the day's ledger opens
- Past days show 🔒, future days show 🔮 and cannot be opened

### The quest log
- Your tasks split into **quests**, shown **one at a time** with ❮ ❯ arrows so you focus on a few things instead of drowning in a wall of checkboxes
- A **live progress bar** that fills as you tick
- **Rank E → D → C → B → A → S**, climbing with your percentage
- **Points**, per-quest counters, and page pips that light up as each quest is cleared
- A **fanfare and "DAY CLEARED"** overlay when you finish everything

### The calendar
- A full-screen month grid; every day carries a **mini progress bar**
- Fully cleared days earn a **gold border and 👑**
- **Days Cleared**, **Month Points** and a live **Streak** counting back from today

### Charts
Three views, each across 7 / 30 / 90 days or all time:
- **Daily** — a bar per day, so you can see the shape of your consistency
- **By Quest** — which quests you clear and which you avoid, ranked
- **By Task** — every single task, grouped by quest, so you can find the exact habit that's slipping

### Multiple dungeons
Run as many separate dungeons as you want — one for work, one for study, one for health. Each keeps its **own quests, theme, streak and history**. They never touch each other.

### Made yours
- **Name, tagline, crest emoji** — anything you like
- **Four themes** — Emerald, Abyss, Ember, Violet
- **Every word of the notification** is editable, including both button labels
- **Rules you choose:**
  - *Previous Days* — **Sealed** (only today) or **Open** (fill in past days)
  - *Answers* — **Editable** or **Locked once ticked** (no take-backs)
  - *Sound* — on or off

### Sound
Real audio, embedded in the file — a glitch-screen hit for pop-ups, a click for buttons, a config tone for panels. Exactly one sound per action, never layered.

### Honest history
Tasks are **dated**. Add a task today and yesterday keeps its old total — a day you cleared perfectly stays perfect forever. Delete a task and it is **retired**, not erased: it vanishes going forward, but the days before still count it. **Your past is never rewritten.**

## Getting started

### 1. Open it
Save `Daily-Survival-Quest-Config.html` anywhere and double-click. It opens in your browser. That's the whole installation.

> **Tip:** bookmark it, or set it as your browser's homepage so it's the first thing you see each morning.

### 2. Forge your first dungeon
On first run you land on the setup screen.

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Quest Name** | `Daily Survival Quest`, or anything you like |
| **Tagline** | One line that means something to you |
| **Crest Emoji** | `⚔️`, `🏔️`, `🔥` — press `Win + .` or right-click → Insert Emoji |
| **Theme** | Click one of the four; the screen recolours instantly |

### 3. Write your quests
A **quest** is a group; a **task** is one thing you did or didn't do.

- **+ Add Task** adds a line to that quest
- **+ Add Quest** adds a new group
- **▲ ▼** reorder, **✕** deletes
- Each row has its own emoji box

Aim for **4–6 quests** of **2–5 tasks** each. Group by theme, not by time of day — something like *The Waking*, *Training*, *The Inner Battle*, *Reset*.

**Write tasks as questions you'd be uncomfortable answering dishonestly.** "Exercise" is weak. "Did I train today?" is a question. That difference is the whole app.

### 4. Set the rules
Pick your **Gate Notification** wording, then choose:
- **Previous Days: Sealed** if you want the pressure to show up daily *(recommended)*
- **Answers: Locked once ticked** if you're the type to quietly undo things

### 5. Save and enter
Hit **Save & Continue**, then **Enter the Gate**. The blur lifts and your calendar appears.

## Your daily loop

1. **Open the app** — ideally at the same time each day
2. **Click today's glowing cell**
3. **Accept the notification** — say Allow
4. **Work through the quests**, ❮ ❯ between them, ticking honestly
5. **Watch the bar fill** and your rank climb
6. **Come back in the evening** and finish what's left

That's it. Two minutes in the morning, two at night.

## Everyday controls

| Where | Icon | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Topbar | 🏛 Archway | Switch between dungeons / create a new one |
| Topbar | 📊 Bars | Open the charts |
| Topbar | ⚙ Sliders | Edit this dungeon |
| Calendar | Prev / Today / Next | Move between months |
| Quest log | ❮ ❯ or ← → | Move between quests |
| Quest log | Pips | Jump straight to a quest |
| Anywhere | `Esc` | Close the pop-up |
| Landing | `Enter` | Enter the gate |

**To add a new dungeon:** archway icon → **+ Forge a New Dungeon**, or ⚙ settings → **+ New Dungeon**.

---

## Good to know

- **Everything is stored in your browser**, tied to that browser on that machine. Copying the HTML file to another computer copies the app, not your history.
- **Don't open it in Incognito** — your record won't survive the session.
- **Erase Everything** wipes all dungeons and all history. It asks first, but there's no undo.
- Your first month will look bad. **That's the data, not a verdict.** Ranks reset daily on purpose — a bad Tuesday has no power over Wednesday.

---

## A last word

You will not clear every day. You are not supposed to. The streak counter is there to be broken and rebuilt, and the honest chart of a real month is jagged, not full.

What matters is that **the day stops passing unnoticed**. That the small good thing you keep meaning to do gets a name, a place, and a box that stays empty if you skip it.

Clear the gate. Then do it again tomorrow.

**⚔️ Your rank is waiting.**
